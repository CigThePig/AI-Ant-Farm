# Limits and unresolved biological claims

This version replaces omniscience, centralized job quotas, generic energy sharing, underground pantries, future nest blueprints and cosmetic scent fields. **It is still an uncalibrated mechanistic model.** A working app and passing tests do not establish scientific accuracy.

## What the implementation establishes

- Individual exploration and local source contact precede resource acquisition.
- Trails are deposited surface signals used in actual worker decisions and degrade without isotropic diffusion.
- Personal route memory is private; return navigation uses observed cell sequences.
- Crop exchanges need sustained spatial encounters. C/P pools, bodies, brood, queen reserves, corpses and metabolic outputs balance separately.
- Egg and brood development occur over biological days. A sealed queen-only scenario uses physiological reserves.
- No central worker quotas or future room plan remain. Removed soil is carried and deposited.
- Temperature and local brood contacts influence state. These response laws remain hypotheses.

## What is approximate or missing

1. Navigation uses exact loop-erased odometry. It does not reproduce visual landmarks, antennal trajectories, noisy path integration, learning curves or all switching behaviour. Memory can be unrealistically precise.
2. Crop C/P are substrate-equivalent accounting variables. Solid prey capture/processing, digestion, excretion, differential assimilation, worker lipid storage and complete nutritional regulation are absent. Protein is an already processed soluble resource. Water is ad libitum.
3. Age, movement, nursing probability, rest, crowding, mortality and task tendencies are not fitted to L. niger distributions. Local rules can cause persistent or inefficient specializations. Adaptive division of labour is not empirically validated.
4. Fecundity is constrained by individual reserves/crop and a provisional maturation interval. It does not model egg batches, ovarian physiology, sperm, unfertilized eggs, male/queen production or mature-colony demography. Seal opening at first emergence is an explicit scenario transition.
5. Stage durations are coarse assumptions, not individual-development datasets. Larvae have no instars. Growth waits for a body-nutrient target; there is no realistic continuous larval consumption curve. Emergence transfers biomass to a new adult without a full eclosion-assistance sequence.
6. The environment is an imposed thermal gradient and moisture fraction. No heat-flow validation, hydraulic transport, gas concentrations, rainfall or structural collapse are modelled. Cold halt is not seasonal diapause.
7. Excavation removes adjacent 2D cells with work accumulation and carries one cell-equivalent load. Moisture/hardness responses are candidate rules. Published mound-assembly pheromones were not assumed to explain tunnelling. No 3D morphology fit, grain size distribution, load-carrying calibration or chamber-width match is claimed.
8. Corpse recognition has a provisional sharp age threshold. Disposal is local exploratory transport outside; no exact chemical, disease transmission, grooming or general waste metabolism is claimed.
9. Populations of 12–24 agents used here do not establish behaviour of colonies with thousands. Spatial worker indexing reduces encounter lookup costs; brood scans and full field decay remain scaling costs. No hybrid population approximation or worker threads are claimed.
10. Browser report export supports seed + initial options + recorded interventions and observations. It is not a bitwise checkpoint restore; historical observations/contact edges are bounded. Manual console mutations and lifecycle-assay preconditioning require the provided scripts to reproduce.
11. The equivalent-playback guarantee applies to identical one-second updates, not arbitrary integrator resolution. Browser catch-up can lag real requested viewing speed. Mobile performance requires real-device confirmation.

## Scientific validation gaps

Experiments A–J report mechanism outcomes and variability. The initial whole-colony C assay is satiation-limited, so its total-harvest differences do not validate a benefit from trails or memory. The controlled assay tests code-level local choice and retained routes; the encoded speed multiplier is not independent evidence of synergy. B is exploratory quality sensitivity, not a quantitative replication of the published crowding experiment. F and J include isolated physiology assays and are explicitly labelled.

External morphology datasets were access-restricted. Primary research access was incomplete for the review, temperature-response paper and Formica study; none supplies fabricated numeric calibration. No p-values, empirical confidence intervals, survival distributions or goodness-of-fit statistics have been invented. Independent raw data, matched experimental apparatus and held-out comparisons remain necessary.
