Scientific model and limits

Workers sense their own cell and neighbours. Successful foragers leave substrate trails; remembered routes belong to individual ants. Crop sharing needs physical encounters.

Stage durations are in biological days. Nutritional rates, digging, mortality, and thermal responses are provisional assumptions. No fitted field-colony prediction is claimed.

Water is supplied ad libitum; seasons, disease, sexual production and realistic 3D architecture are outside this model.
